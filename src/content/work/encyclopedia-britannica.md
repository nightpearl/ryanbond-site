**Encyclopedia Britannica’s** commitment to rigor, research, fact-checking, and editing is the prevailing reason they remain the pivotal place of knowledge; celebrating students and lifelong learners. The product enabled users to explore over 250-years of fact-checked, insightful articles, biographies, and interactive features, curated from the rich knowledge of leading contributors from around the globe and thoroughly vetted with a meticulous editorial process.

My role as **Director of Consumer Products** was to manage a **multi-discplined team** in executing on the **vision**, **roadmap**, **direction and daily execution** I provided for the 50+ individuals resourced on Britannica.com.

The team consisted of Product Managers, User Experience Designers, User Researchers, Visual Designers, Front End / Back End Developers, Quality Assurance Testors, Technology/Systems Leads, Editorial & Content Strategists, Analytics, SEO, AdOps and Marketing/Social.

The work on Britannica.com included **optimizations, enhancements and redesigns** in order to modernize, monetize and insure high performance across Desktop/Tablet/Mobile, SEO, AdOps and UX/UI. The product was driven by human centered design princicples via design sprints, A/B testing and analytics in order to maximize user and business impact.

As the Product Manager I developed project timelines, gathered requirements to successfully meet key milestones, staffed and budged, analyzed and reported project information to stakeholders cross departmentally for concise communications. **Performance metrics available upon request.**
