## The challenge

Big brands need campaigns that span web, social, video, gaming and packaging, delivered on time by teams spread across cities, agencies and vendors.

## What I did

At Razorfish I grew from Project Lead to Program Manager, running retainers of **$15M+** and individual projects of **$3M+** for clients including Kraft, Kellogg's, MillerCoors, McDonald's, AT&T, Dell, State Farm, JCPenney, US Cellular, General Electric and Microsoft. I wrote SOWs, managed budgets and burn, and led onshore, offshore and vendor teams of 10 to 60+ people. I also mentored project managers and helped build the agency's delivery practices.

## A favorite: Cheez-It "Vote for the Top Cheese"

During an election year, how do you get loyal snackers to try new flavors and bring their friends along? We ran a mock election where cheese "candidates" campaigned for your vote, and flip-flopping was encouraged. Fans earned votes by playing games and sharing, across Facebook, YouTube, PlayStation, ads and packaging.

- **25% sales increase** across featured flavors
- **65 million+** total votes from **620,000+** voters
- **600,000+** new Facebook fans

I also managed the Windows 8.1 and Surface Pro launch advertising for Microsoft, across video, pre-roll and display.
