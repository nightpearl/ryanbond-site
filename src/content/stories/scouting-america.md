## The challenge

Scouting runs on volunteers and lean staff. Councils have to recruit families, raise funds and communicate across print, email, social and the web, usually without a full marketing department.

## What I did

I've worked with Scouting from every side of the table:

- **Strategist.** I provide strategic guidance on print, email, social and video campaigns and on website redesigns for the council.
- **Producer.** I produce promotional, informational and fundraising videos end to end, from concept and shooting through the final edit.
- **Builder.** I've built internal tools that help non-marketing staff do their own communications work, without waiting on a specialist.
- **Leader.** For more than 15 years I've served as a Cubmaster, Assistant Scoutmaster, Scoutmaster and District Committee Member, working directly with young people and their families.

## Why it matters

This is where I see most clearly what a product really is: something that has to work for a busy parent, a volunteer with ten minutes, and a staff member doing three jobs at once. The same principle runs through everything else on this site. Start with the people you're serving, and make the right thing the easy thing.
