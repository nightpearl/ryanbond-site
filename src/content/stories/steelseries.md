## The challenge

Gamers expect their software to keep up with the games they play. SteelSeries needed its GG software to keep earning a place on players' machines, in a market where a better alternative is always one download away.

## What I did

I owned the full product lifecycle for two lines of the SteelSeries GG platform:

- **GG Moments**, which lets players record, edit, review and share PC gameplay highlights.
- **GG QuickSet**, which customizes SteelSeries hardware and keeps it compatible across games.

I ran discovery and delivery side by side. Qualitative signals from Discord and user surveys, combined with NPS and usage data, fed straight into prioritization. That kept the team pointed at outcomes rather than a fixed roadmap, while I led design, engineering and QA through bi-weekly releases tied to quarterly strategy.

I also partnered with executive, creative, marketing and ecosystem teams on product strategy, key alliances, and acquisition and retention campaigns.

## What changed

A steady, predictable release rhythm, continuous feature rollouts that sustained engagement with GG Moments, and smoother hardware and software workflows that improved compatibility and adoption across gaming ecosystems.
