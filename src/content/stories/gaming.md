## Where it started

For ten years I was Executive Game Producer, Program Manager and "Kid Advocate" for **Postopia**, Kraft and Post Cereals' online entertainment platform for kids. The launch team was 55+ people across five cities. I owned everything about the games: strategy, budgets, vendor selection, concept, production, testing and marketing.

Across Postopia, Nabiscoworld and Candystand I produced **150+ games** in Flash, Shockwave, WildTangent and Unity: arcade, puzzle, sports, adventure, multiplayer and virtual pets. They featured our own characters, cereal mascots like Fred and Barney, and licensed partners including Batman, Justice League, Teen Titans and The Polar Express. I also served as a child marketing and insights advisor to Kraft.

## Where it went

- **Guardians of History** (Britannica): a voice adventure for Alexa and Google that teaches history through play, with up to 40 minutes of gameplay, eight endings and a full voice cast.
- **SteelSeries GG Moments**: software that lets PC gamers capture and share their best plays.
- **Geek Bar Chicago**, a gaming and fandom venue, and marketing for the **Firefly: A Fistful of Credits** board game.

## What I learned

Games are the most honest product feedback there is. If something isn't fun, players leave right away. That instinct for fast, honest feedback loops shapes how I build every product.
