## The challenge

Early-stage teams and mission-driven organizations often know the problem they want to solve, but not yet what to build, for whom, or in what order. They need senior product judgment without a full-time hire, and they need it to move fast.

## What I did

- **An AI health app for older adults.** For a venture-backed startup building an LLM-powered health app for seniors, their families and caregivers, I built the roadmap and set priority features, then guided the product from concept through Alpha and Beta toward a subscription launch.
- **One front door for a hospital group.** I led product management to unify three separate hospital websites into a single HIPAA-compliant patient platform, designed to make care easier to find and to grow appointment volume and patient engagement.
- **Early-stage ventures.** I've guided several founders through rapid concepting, investor pitches and high-fidelity MVPs.

## How AI changes the work

I use Claude, Figma Make, Lovable and similar tools to turn customer interviews into user stories, and ideas into clickable prototypes, in days instead of weeks. The speed is new. The discipline isn't: talk to customers, test the riskiest assumption first, and decide what *not* to build.

## What changed

Clients walk away with a direction they can defend: a prioritized roadmap grounded in real customer insight, and a prototype they can put in front of users or investors.
