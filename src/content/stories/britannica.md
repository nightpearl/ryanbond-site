## The challenge

Britannica had the most trusted reference content in the world, but its consumer products hadn't kept pace with how families and students actually look things up. Britannica Kids had barely changed in over a decade.

## What I did

I owned vision, roadmap, UX and P&L for Britannica's consumer products, leading product managers, UX and design, and setting direction for a 50+ person team across engineering, editorial, game design and marketing.

- **Started with the user.** We surveyed kids, parents and educators, built user profiles, and ran content and UX audits before deciding what to keep, improve or cut.
- **Changed how the team worked.** I introduced Agile practices and stood up research, analytics and SEO teams, moving the organization from shipping projects to owning outcomes.
- **Shipped fast by reusing what worked.** The Britannica Kids relaunch borrowed proven content and infrastructure from sister products to get to market quickly.

## What changed

The redesigned Britannica Kids **doubled subscription revenue in its first year**. Alongside it we overhauled Britannica.com and launched four new product lines, including the Britannica Insights browser extension, the Curiosity Compass campaign for Britannica's 250th anniversary, the company's first voice and IoT integration with Samsung Bixby, and **Guardians of History**, a voice game recognized among Voicebot's Top 20 Brand Innovators in Voice for 2019.
